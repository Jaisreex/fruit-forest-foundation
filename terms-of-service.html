About FruitForest Foundation

FruitForest Foundation is a non-profit organization focused on:

  • Planting fruit trees across the world
  • Reducing hunger and restoring ecosystems
  • Supporting environmental sustainability
  • Promoting wellness and wealth education
  • Encouraging community participation

Mission:

  • To plant fruit trees across the world to reduce hunger and restore ecosystems
  • To empower people through wealth education and wellness

Goal:

  • Create abundant, self-sustaining food sources for communities everywhere
  • Build financially educated, healthy, and self-reliant societies
